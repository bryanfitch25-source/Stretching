# Suggested Google Play store listing copy

## Title (≤30 chars)
`Yield: Recipe Scaler` (21 chars)

## Short description (≤80 chars)
`Scale any recipe by servings or pan size. Offline, no account needed.` (71 chars)

## Full description (≤4000 chars)

Yield is the recipe box for anyone who actually cooks: big tap targets for
messy hands, a phone propped on the counter, and math you never have to do
yourself.

**Scale by servings.**
Bump the stepper up or down and every ingredient rescales instantly —
proportionally, down to the fraction. Doubling a chili for guests or
halving a cake for two? Yield does the arithmetic so you don't have to
eyeball it.

**Scale by pan size.**
Baking in a different pan than the recipe calls for? Enter your source pan
and your target pan (round or rectangular) and Yield scales every
ingredient by the actual area ratio — not a guess.

**Sensible units, not robotic conversions.**
Toggle between imperial and metric and Yield rounds the way a real cook
would: to the nearest 1/8 for cups and spoons, to the nearest gram for
metric — never a fraction nobody could measure.

**Shopping list, built for you.**
Pick a few recipes you're planning to cook, and Yield combines their
scaled ingredients into one shopping list — automatically adding up
matching ingredients across recipes. Check things off as you shop, or
export the whole list as plain text to send to Notes, Messages, or
anywhere else.

**Everything stays on your phone.**
No account, no sign-in, no internet connection required. Your recipes are
stored only on your device and are yours alone.

**Built for the kitchen, not a boardroom.**
Warm colors, a readable serif for titles, and giant tap targets — designed
to be glanced at and tapped once with a flour-dusted thumb, not squinted
at across the room.

What's inside:
• Add, edit, and delete your own recipes
• Live servings stepper that rescales every ingredient
• Pan-size scaling by area (round or rectangular pans)
• Imperial ⇄ metric conversion with sensible rounding
• Combined, deduplicated shopping lists across multiple recipes
• Share/export shopping lists as plain text
• Full dark mode support
• 100% offline, no account, no ads, no tracking

Yield doesn't try to be a social network, a meal-planning subscription, or
a recipe-discovery feed. It's a scale, for recipes. That's it.
