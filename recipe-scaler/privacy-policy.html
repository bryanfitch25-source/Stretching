Yield ("the app") is a recipe-scaling tool. This policy explains what happens to your data — in short: nothing leaves your device.

What data the app stores

The recipes, ingredients, quantities, and instructions you enter are stored only in the app's local storage on your Android device (via the Capacitor Preferences API). This includes any pan sizes, servings, or unit preferences you set.

What we collect

Nothing. Yield has no backend server, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no user accounts. We do not collect, transmit, or have access to any of your data, usage patterns, or device information. The app does not require an internet connection to function.

Sharing your data

The app includes an optional "Share" feature for exporting a shopping list as plain text. This action is entirely user-initiated: it hands the text to Android's native share sheet, and it is your choice which app (Notes, Messages, Email, etc.) you send it to. Yield itself never sends this data anywhere on its own.

Data deletion

You can delete individual recipes at any time from within the app. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data, since nothing is backed up outside your device unless your device's own Android backup settings do so (a setting controlled by you and Google, not by this app).

Children's privacy

Yield does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.
